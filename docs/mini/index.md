# MINI

MINI is the planned economic system covering the Genesis Pool, MINI Lucky, and the MiniJAM ecosystem.

## Genesis Pool

The Genesis Pool is a planned part of the MINI system. Its final parameters and rules are not established by this overview.

## MINI Lucky

MINI Lucky is a planned product area within the ecosystem. Details will be documented when the implementation and formal rules are published.

## Economic System

The economic design may change as the project develops. Once on-chain contracts and formal rules are published, those on-chain rules take precedence.
